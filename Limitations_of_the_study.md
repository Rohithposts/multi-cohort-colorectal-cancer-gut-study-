There are certain limitations that must be kept in mind when viewing this project. They are mentioned below:

1) While the entire aim of the study is to find robust signals that survive mutliple confounders and batch effects, there is still a possibility that these results might just be noise or these same confounders making a pattern.
  
2) These results are specific to the selcted samples/cohorts/studies. A different set of 5 cohorts might yield different results.

3) There are multiple confounders involved, and accounting and adjusting for all of them is beyond the scope of this project.

4) While the taxa levels were analysed at genus level due to this being the lowest possible level where both 16S and WGS reads can give us information about, 
different analysis pipelines and different tools were used, that might have influenced the results.
